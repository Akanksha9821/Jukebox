ABOUT THIS PROTOTYPE

How to listen to Earth

Earth Jukebox turns published Earth science measurements into synthesized notes in your browser. The same data powers the live graph and the visualization. All sound mappings are editable.

Scientific mode encodes measured values along a continuous pitch range. Musical mode snaps them to a pentatonic scale to make a more melodic result. These are mappings, not literal sounds Earth makes.

Use Tab to move between controls. Use Space on Play, arrow keys on sliders, and Describe to hear the current reading spoken. Playback and recording require a browser that supports the Web Audio API.

The NASA imagery is a visual reference and is not synchronized by observation date. Provenance and limitations are documented in DATA_SOURCES.md.
